# Agent Rules and Guidelines

This document outlines rules and guidelines for agents interacting with this codebase.

## General
- When working on tasks outlined in `TODO.md`, ensure that items are marked as completed (`[X]`) as they are finished. If a task is not present in `TODO.md` but is a logical step towards completing a larger goal, consider adding it to the relevant section of `TODO.md` and then marking it as complete.
